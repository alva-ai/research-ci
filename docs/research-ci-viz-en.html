System boundary

The Engine checks mechanics and records evidence. The Lab owns research meaning. Neither side silently takes over the other's job.

ENGINE · deterministic

Protocol, checks, and review decisions

  • Stage order, legal transitions, and approved-upstream access
  • DataSet, FeatureSet, SignalSet, AlgoSet
  • Fixed baseline and stage-scoped eval menu
  • Four-slot runner, reports, Cards, manifests
  • Actionable refusals, stable Card ID, and review provenance

Sources, research, and case semantics

  • Downloads, source inspection, and joins
  • Features, labels, candidates, and models

  • Case rules and their counterexamples
  • Portfolio construction and external backtests

One enforced Agent/reviewer loop

Eval pass/fail belongs to an attempt. Workflow state only says who may act next, and the CLI refuses every skipped transition with the exact next legal command.

Current stateActorLegal actionResult
readyAgentrun as often as neededAppend a numbered valid or invalid attempt; remain ready

readyAgentsubmit one valid attempt with a recommendationPublish the formal candidate and enter review

reviewReviewerreject --message ...Return to ready; the Agent must create a new attempt
approved / stoppedHumanreopen --message ...Return to ready and lock all downstream stages
Agent handoff: each submitted Card carries only continue, stop, or uncertain as its recommendation. The reviewer owns the transition.

The same four slots at every data stage

All slots evaluate the formal main table and named sidecars. Eval columns never become part of the handoff table.

Slot 1 · Engine

Baseline

Fixed correctness floor: schema, key grid, finite values, source preservation, and stage protocol coverage.

Slot 2 · Selected

Library

The external Agent chooses registered general methods from this stage's menu and records why each applies.

Slot 3 · Lab

Case

Task-specific definitions and assumptions, preferably Polars expressions, with custom runners where required.

Slot 4 · Counterexample

Case eval

Valid fixtures pass; invalid inputs and counterexamples must make the matching Slot 3 rule fail.

Sig: the full inner research loop

Sig owns target-aware selection and prediction construction. It must account for every approved feature before proposing the small final handoff to Algo.

Formal handoff

  • sig/main.parquet
  • keys + instrument/price context
  • final signals only
  • direct Algo input

Research evidence

  • screening.parquet
  • candidates.parquet
  • redundancy + clusters
  • disposition + lineage

Development context

  • context/label.parquet
  • sample/block metadata
  • fitted artifacts
  • never appended to main

Optional confirmation

  • validation_plan.json
  • frozen source + products
  • disjoint holdout keys
  • final signals only

Earlier draft and current MVP

The current MVP keeps the inspectable stage discipline while avoiding guarantees it has not implemented.

Design concernEarlier draftCurrent MVP
Research boundaryLarge up-front contract with outcomes, horizons, power, alternatives, and holdout tiersSmall mission/scope/invariants contract; labels and recipes emerge in later stages
Per-layer structureFour slots with a decisive statistical floor and lab vetoesFour slots for correctness, selected general evidence, case definitions, and counterexamples
Formal outputsTyped layer outputs and cardsOne main Parquet per stage plus explicit sidecars, report, Card, and manifest
SigPlanned score/event layerImplemented full-universe screening, dispositions, candidate/final lineage, label sidecars, and direct Algo handoff
AlgoPlanned consumer plugin and simulatorImplemented weights/trades contracts with an injected external backtester
Sig holdout, freeze, and confirmationCore design, partially implementedOptional one-time protocol implemented; secure process custody remains planned
Universal alpha verdictNull worlds, calibrated thresholds, trial countsEvidence and explicit review; no single automatic alpha verdict
Current boundary: an approved Sig is ready for Algo research, and an approved Algo is a reviewed strategy/backtest artifact. Neither approval is a capital or live-trading authorization.
